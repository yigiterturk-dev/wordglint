# App Store listing — WordSpark (en-US, iOS v1)

**App name (30):** WordSpark: English Vocabulary

**Subtitle (30):** Vocabulary in 60-second rounds

**Promotional text (170):**
Academic & everyday English vocabulary in 60-second rounds. Offline.

**Full description (4000):**
WordSpark turns vocabulary practice into 60-second rounds you can play with one
hand, on the bus, with no connection.

Words flash past. You catch them — put them back in order, decide whether you saw
them, pick the meaning, or type the word from its definition. Memory and vocabulary
sharpen at the same time.

WORD PACKS BUILT ON PUBLISHED RESEARCH
• Everyday English — the New General Service List, 2,712 core words that cover the
  great majority of general English
• Academic Core — Coxhead's Academic Word List, 570 word families, the backbone of
  university reading and writing
• IELTS & TOEFL — the New Academic Word List, 963 words drawn from academic texts

Every word comes with a plain English definition, a natural example sentence, and a
difficulty level. No invented word lists.

FIVE WAYS TO PRACTISE
⚡ Recall the Order — rebuild the sequence you just saw
🎯 Was It There? — swipe seen / not seen
🧠 Pick the Meaning — four options, one right
✍️ Type It Out — meaning in, word out
⏱ Beat the Clock — as many as you can in 60 seconds

📝 Mock Test — 20 mixed questions with a breakdown by question type

BUILT TO KEEP YOU COMING BACK
• Daily Spark — the same deck for everyone, every day
• Streaks, levels, XP, 16 badges
• Weak Words deck — everything you got wrong, from every pack
• Starred words and a searchable dictionary with per-word progress

ENGLISH OR TURKISH
Meanings and the whole interface switch between English definitions and Turkish
translations. Learning English from Turkish? Turn on Turkish. Studying in English?
Leave it in English.

WORKS OFFLINE
Everything is on your device. No account, no sign-up, no tracking.
